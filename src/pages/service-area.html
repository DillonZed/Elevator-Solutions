Where We Work

ESI is based in downtown Vancouver. Most of our work is in the Lower Mainland, and we take on projects across British Columbia and in Alberta.

Lower Mainland

Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey, New Westminster, the North Shore and the Tri-Cities. This is where most of our work is.

Fraser Valley

Langley, Abbotsford, Chilliwack and surrounding communities.

Vancouver Island

Victoria, Nanaimo and communities up the Island.

Okanagan & Interior

Kelowna, Kamloops, Vernon, Penticton and the wider Interior.

Northern BC

Prince George and communities across the north.

Alberta

Calgary, Edmonton and other Alberta communities, depending on the project. In Alberta, condominium planning uses reserve fund studies rather than depreciation reports.
