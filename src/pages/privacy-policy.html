Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

ESI Elevator Solutions Inc. ("ESI", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through elevatorsolutions.ca and how we use it.

What we collect

When you contact us through the website, by email or by phone, we collect the information you choose to give us: your name, email address, phone number, company or building name, building location, details about your elevators, and any documents you send, such as a depreciation report or maintenance contract.

Like most websites, we also collect basic usage data, such as pages visited, browser type and the site that referred you, through cookies and analytics tools.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, prepare quotes, deliver our services and keep records of our work with you. We use website usage data to understand how the site is used and improve it. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run our business: a customer relationship management platform to receive and manage inquiries, email and document storage, website hosting, and website analytics. These providers process information on our behalf and may store it on servers outside Canada, including in the United States. We do not share your information with elevator contractors or other third parties without your permission, unless required by law.

Cookies

The site uses cookies for analytics and to make the contact form work. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work as intended if you do.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or to meet legal and business record-keeping requirements.
