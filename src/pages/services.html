Specifications & tendering

Getting competitive pricing on elevator work

Whether it is a new maintenance contract or a full modernization, we can prepare the specification and RFP, put the work out to tender, compare the bids and help you select a contractor. Every contractor prices the same scope, so the bids can be compared fairly.

Tendering is usually part of a modernization or maintenance contract review, and we can also do it on its own.

Not sure where to start?

Most clients start with a condition assessment

If the elevators are getting older, breaking down more often, or showing up as a large number in your depreciation report, a condition assessment is usually the right first step. It tells you what you have and what to plan for.
