About ESI

On the side of the people who pay for elevators

ESI Elevator Solutions Inc. is a Vancouver-based elevator consulting firm. There is an information gap between the people who pay for elevators and the companies that sell and service them, and ESI exists to close it.

Our team advises building owners, strata corporations and property managers so their projects are done properly, on time and at a fair price. We have no equipment to sell and no maintenance contracts to protect.

The team

The people behind ESI

ESI has two divisions: elevator consulting for building owners and managers, and ESI Elevator Rescue Training for emergency responders. Engineering work is delivered with our partners at Kron Engineering.

Glenn Zolnierczyk, CEI, PMP®

Principal and Senior Consultant

Glenn has more than 35 years of elevator industry experience. He has been a NAESA International QEI certified elevator inspector (C-3619) in good standing since 2007, and a Project Management Professional (PMP® #1619378) with the Project Management Institute since June 2013.

Glenn also leads ESI Elevator Rescue Training, which trains firefighters, hospital staff and industrial responders to rescue people trapped in elevators.

Cori Williams

Project Manager

Cori oversees project coordination and administration at ESI Elevator Solutions. She works closely with clients, contractors and project stakeholders to coordinate schedules, monitor project progress and keep communication clear throughout each project. Her focus is on keeping projects organized, running efficiently and meeting client expectations.

Radu Gnasienco, P.Eng

Professional Engineer, Kron Engineering

Radu has over 28 years of engineering experience. He prepares technical documentation for the modernization and installation of vertical transportation equipment in residential and commercial buildings. ESI's engineering services are delivered with Kron Engineering.

Dillon Zolnierczyk, BBA

Business Development

Dillon started his career in marketing in the commercial finance industry. At ESI he focuses on simple technical solutions that improve communication and transparency between everyone involved in an elevator project.
