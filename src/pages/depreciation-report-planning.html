Why the elevator line needs a closer look

Elevators are one of the largest costs your reserve fund has to cover

A depreciation report has to cover dozens of building components, so each one gets a general estimate. Elevators are harder to estimate than most. Two elevators of the same age can be years apart in condition, depending on how they have been maintained and whether parts are still made for them.

If the timing or the cost of a modernization is off, the funding plan built on it is off too. Our assessment looks only at the elevators and gives you numbers to plan with.

What the planning report covers

Specific to your elevators

Condition

The state of the controller, machine, hoistway equipment, doors and car, from an on-site review.

Remaining life

A component-by-component estimate of how long each part can reasonably keep running.

Obsolescence & parts

Whether replacement parts are still available, and how that affects repair times and modernization timing.

Maintenance

How well the equipment is being maintained, and whether the maintenance contract fits the building.

Modernization timing

When a modernization makes sense, and what can reasonably wait.

Capital budget

Realistic costs for the work ahead, laid out for your planning and funding discussions.

The rules in brief

BC depreciation reports since 2024

  • Strata corporations with five or more lots must get a depreciation report at least every five years.
  • Since July 1, 2024, owners can no longer vote to waive the requirement.
  • Reports look ahead 30 years and include funding models for the reserve fund.
  • In Alberta, condominium corporations must update a reserve fund study at least every five years.
  • In Saskatchewan, condominium corporations with 12 or more units must update a reserve fund study at least every five years, looking ahead 25 years.

Sources: Province of British Columbia, strata legislation changes · Government of Alberta, reserve funds · CMHC, Saskatchewan condominium fact sheet. This is a summary, not legal advice.

ESI does not prepare depreciation reports or reserve fund studies. Those cover the whole building and are prepared by qualified providers. We do the detailed elevator work that sits alongside them.
