For strata councils & property managers

Got your depreciation report back?

A depreciation report covers the whole building, so the elevator line is usually a broad estimate. Send us the report. We'll review the elevator portion and quote a detailed condition assessment and planning report: remaining life, obsolescence, parts availability, maintenance and modernization timing.

What an elevator consultant does →

An independent opinion

An elevator contractor's goals are different from yours. We have no equipment to sell and no maintenance contract to protect, so our job is to find the problems and tell you plainly.

Protect a major investment

Elevators are among the most expensive systems in a building. We tell you how well yours are being maintained and what that means for their life.

Fewer shutdowns

Better maintenance improves reliability, cuts tenant complaints and reduces liability exposure. We check the work is being done.

Budget with confidence

Know whether it is time to plan a modernization, or time for a new service provider, with a fair estimate of the remaining life.
